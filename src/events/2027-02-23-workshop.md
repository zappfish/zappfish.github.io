**Workshop Goals**

* Introduce the ZAPP annotation tools and teach community members how to use them
* Collect data for the Atlas through hands-on sessions
* Gather feedback about the annotation tools and the Atlas
* Discuss curation guidelines and data governance

**Who Can Attend**

Anyone in the zebrafish community is welcome to join in person. Some parts of the workshop will also be offered virtually via Zoom, but all hands-on sessions are for in-person participants only.

**What to Bring**

This is a hands-on workshop. Participants will bring their own images and data and annotate them using the ZAPP annotation tools.

* Images (phenotypic fish and controls) and information from at least 5 individual chemical treatment experiments (different chemicals and/or different concentrations)
* Data can be already published or new data that has not yet been made public
* You decide whether to publish your data in the publicly accessible Atlas or keep it unpublished within the annotation tool platform. See our [data governance rules](/ZAPPGovernance/data-governance/) for how data sharing and ownership work.
